The job we want to make easier

An agent comparing software needs to distinguish a monthly subscription from a monthly equivalent billed annually, identify seat minimums, and leave unpublished prices unknown. Fetching a page is only part of that job.

Facts with evidence

Each returned field should point to its source and supporting text, including when and under which conditions the page was read.

Limits you can use

The agent should set a maximum data age, response deadline and price before requesting a result.

Unknown means unknown

Missing, stale or conflicting information should produce explicit statuses, never an invented price or a silent zero.

Help test whether this is worth building

We are looking for developers building procurement, research or competitive-intelligence agents. Bring one recurring task, 3–5 public pricing URLs, and the fields your agent needs. Do not send credentials, private pages or customer data.

We will scope a small sample together and compare the approach against your existing retrieval setup. We have not established an accuracy, speed or cost advantage. The pilot does not require payment.

Email hello@aispenddoctor.com with your task, freshness requirement and the failure you want to fix.

A focused first step

The proposed first scope is publicly accessible SaaS pricing pages with explicit plan prices. Authenticated sites, paywalls, negotiated quotes, purchases and unrestricted web access are outside this pilot. Later access to restricted data would need agreements with its providers.

AI Spend Doctor's free CSV cost checker is available separately. This page describes a new research direction.
