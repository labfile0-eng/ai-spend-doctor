Claude Sonnet 4 vs Sonnet 5 price: cheaper per token, closer per task

Short answer: Claude Sonnet 4, 4.5 and 4.6 cost $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. Claude Sonnet 5 costs $2 and $10, a third less per token. Sonnet 5 uses a newer tokenizer that produces about 30% more tokens for the same text, so for the same work the list-price difference is about 13%, not 33%.

Price per token is not price per task. Anthropic notes that Claude 4.7 and later models use a newer tokenizer that produces about 30% more tokens for the same text, depending on the content (pricing page). Output length and quality on your own tasks also change the result, so treat the numbers below as a scenario and test on your own prompts.

A worked scenario

At $6,000 a month on Sonnet 4.x, the same work at Sonnet 5 list prices with 30% more tokens would be about $6,000 × 2/3 × 1.3 ≈ $5,200. The difference, around $800 a month, depends on your content and output length, so measure it on your own prompts.

What to do before switching

  1. Run a sample of real prompts on both models and compare quality and token counts.
  2. Check whether your prompts are cached; cache reads are also cheaper on Sonnet 5 ($0.20 versus $0.30).
  3. Switch one service first and compare the next week's bill.

Questions

How much cheaper is Claude Sonnet 5 than Sonnet 4?

Per token, a third cheaper: $2 versus $3 per million input tokens and $10 versus $15 per million output tokens. Sonnet 5 uses a newer tokenizer that produces about 30% more tokens for the same text, so for the same work the list-price difference is about 13%.

Should I switch from Sonnet 4 to Sonnet 5?

Decide on your own evaluation: compare quality and token counts on a sample of real prompts. The price difference per task is smaller than the per-token prices suggest.
