03 / NOTHING TO HAND OVER

What happens on your device
stays on your device.

  • Nothing is sent. The page's security policy forbids it from making any network request (connect-src 'none'), and your browser enforces that, not us.
  • Works offline. Load the page, turn off your internet, then open your file. The check still runs.
  • Nothing to hand over. No account, no API key, no analytics, nothing stored after you close the tab.
  • Source available. Read every line on GitHub.
